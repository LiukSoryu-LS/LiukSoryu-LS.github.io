# About Liuk Soryu

The games that shaped my childhood? **Final Fantasy IX, The Legend of Dragoon, and Kingdom Hearts.** 💜

I've been an RPG lover through and through ever since. I'm also a **beginner MTG player**, and I have a deep passion for games with intricate lore, fascinating characters, and worlds that feel like there's always something more to discover.

As for me?

**A little bit messy. A little bit fruity. A little bit shy.**

But always curious, always passionate, and probably overthinking some piece of game lore somewhere. 😂

I have a pretty strong sense of morality and I can't stand injustice or discrimination. I want this to be a space where people can enjoy games, talk about stories, share theories, and just be themselves without feeling judged.

And then there's the streaming...

**Stream mayhem is never the same twice.**

Sometimes there's a plan. Sometimes there absolutely isn't. Sometimes everything works perfectly.

And sometimes... well... **we improvise.** 😂

So be prepared for the unexpected.
