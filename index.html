ABOUT LIUKSORYU

Hey! I’m LiukSoryu — a gacha gamer, story enjoyer, artist, streamer and professional overthinker.

This space is where I bring together the things I enjoy most: gacha games, character builds, lore, theories, creative experiments and live community chaos.

You’ll find a mix of videos, streams, guides, podcasts, discussions and the occasional completely unnecessary deep-dive into a fictional character.

My goal is simple: make gaming content that feels like hanging out with someone who genuinely loves these games.

I’m especially interested in the worlds behind the characters — their stories, motivations, relationships and the little details that make a game worth obsessing over.

This site will gradually grow into a library of guides, infographics, theories and community projects alongside the channel.

So whether you’re here for a build, a lore rabbit hole, a stream, or just to see what chaos is happening today — welcome.
