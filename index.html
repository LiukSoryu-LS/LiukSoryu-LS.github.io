About LIUKSORYU

LIUKSORYU is a gacha gaming space built around the things that keep these games interesting long after the pull screen: stories, characters, theories, guides and community.

The goal is simple — make content that feels thoughtful without losing the fun of gaming. Some days that means breaking down a character kit. Other days it means chasing a strange lore connection or asking a question the game may not want us to ask.

This is also a space for people who enjoy games without needing to fit into one particular corner of the community. Everyone is welcome here as long as we keep it respectful.

Expect a mix of videos, live streams, character guides, lore discussions, theories and gaming conversations across the games currently on the channel.

Thanks for being here. 💜
